# Figure brief 09 · Model hierarchy for electrochemical corrosion

[Figure index](README.md) · [Course home](../README.md)

**Status:** original design proposal, not a rendered image or computed result.

## Layout
Five panels: isolated molecule; neutral vacuum slab; implicit solvent; explicit interfacial water/ions; potential-controlled model. Show what each newly adds and what remains unspecified.

## Required labels and explanation
At the end include an electron reservoir and a clearly stated countercharge/electrolyte model. Distinguish fixed N from fixed mu_e and show a separate proton/ion reservoir.

## Scientific safeguards
A cartoon electrolyte is not proof of realistic ion concentration or potential. Do not claim that adding water alone controls the electrode potential.
