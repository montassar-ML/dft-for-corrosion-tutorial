# Figure brief 03 · Fe(110) slab and adsorbate model card

[Figure index](README.md) · [Course home](../README.md)

**Status:** original design proposal, not a rendered image or computed result.

## Layout
Use top and side views of a crystallographically checked bcc(110) slab when actual coordinates are available. Label in-plane lattice vectors, surface normal, active/fixed layers, vacuum and periodic cell. Place a labeled imidazole with the two N environments distinguished.

## Required labels and explanation
Indicate candidate top, short-bridge and long-bridge regions on the actual lattice. Label all contacts as initial candidates until relaxed geometries exist. Show a separate clean-surface reference.

## Scientific safeguards
Without checked coordinates, use a block schematic labeled “not an atomic structure.” Do not manufacture a supposedly relaxed Fe–N distance or lattice constant.

## Proposed caption
Original schematic based on concepts discussed in Refs. [47](../README.md#ref-47) [44](../README.md#ref-44). Fe(110) slab and adsorbate model card. Intended for conceptual teaching; not a reproduction of a journal figure and not a result of the proposed Fe(110) case study.

## Accessible production specification
Use a clean white background, legible high-contrast labels and generous spacing. Do not rely only on color: label signs, species and arrows directly. Provide alt text describing the conceptual comparison. Retain editable vector source when drawn. If future actual data are used, add provenance, units, numerical settings and permissions and remove any ambiguity between results and schematic content.
