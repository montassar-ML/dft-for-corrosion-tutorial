## Literature-reading guidance
The bibliography contains useful examples of real surface and electrochemical analyses. Consult the original article to inspect its figure and methods; do not extract an image from a paywalled or merely viewable page and assume redistribution is allowed. A conceptual figure drawn from first principles is different from tracing a published figure. Preserve attribution and obtain permission or a compatible license for any future reproduction.
