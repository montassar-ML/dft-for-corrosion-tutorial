**Status:** original design proposal, not a rendered image or computed result.

## Layout
Left: an isolated generic labeled inhibitor with discrete orbital bars. Right: a repeated surface cell with a molecular adsorbate and a continuous schematic band/DOS panel. Under each, write what the model contains and omits.

## Required labels and explanation
Molecular: species, conformer, IP/EA, local descriptors. Periodic: facet, site, coverage, spin and electrostatics. A third small strip shows the electrolyte/potential layer required for a real electrode model.

## Scientific safeguards
Discrete levels and DOS shape are conceptual, not calculated. Do not imply that the molecular gap becomes a gap of the metallic interface.
